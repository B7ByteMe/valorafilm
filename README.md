### Advanced Ad-Free Media Streaming Experience

**An open-source Android application natively built for performance and immersive media playback.**

</div>

---

## Features

* **Stream and Download Ad-Free** - Enjoy media without interruptions.
* **Multi Audio & External Subs Support** - Customize your playback experience.
* **WatchList** - Keep track of your favorite movies and shows.
* **External Player & Downloader Support** - Use your preferred playback and download tools.
* **Sync with Desktop App** - Seamlessly continue your experience between mobile and desktop.
* **Create Custom Sources** - Build and integrate your own media providers.
* **In-App APK Updater** - Download and install new app versions directly from within Valora Film.
* **Live Update Progress** - Monitor APK download progress in real time.
* **Optimized Startup Buffer** - Faster video playback with an optimized initial buffer.
* **Modern Settings Interface** - Clean and refined settings interface with transparent cards.
